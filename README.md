# Messier Chart Builder

**Created by SaltyPogue** · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/saltypogue) · [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/saltypogue) · [X](https://x.com/saltypogue)

Build a chart of all 110 Messier objects from your own astrophotos. Drag your photos onto the grid and the chart updates and saves itself after every photo.

## Download for Mac

👉 **[Get the latest Mac version](../../releases/latest)**

- **Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4…)**, most Macs from late 2020 on: download `Messier-Chart-Builder-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip`
- **Intel Mac**: download `Messier-Chart-Builder-Mac-Intel.zip`

Not sure which you have? Apple menu  → **About This Mac** → look at **Chip** or **Processor**.

### Opening it the first time

1. Double-click the downloaded zip, then drag **Messier Chart Builder** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the app, because it isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Messier Chart Builder.

You only need to do this once.

## Using it

- **Drag photos onto the window.** Files named with their number (`M31.jpg`, `m042_orion.tif`, `Messier 81.png`) go to the right object automatically. You can drop many at once, or a whole folder.
- **Click** a square to pick a photo for it. **Right-click** (or Control-click) to replace or remove it.
- **Export chart…** saves a PNG, JPG or TIFF. The chart is also saved automatically to `~/Messier Chart/messier_chart.png`.
- **Edit title…**, **Clear all…** and **Star border: On/Off** are in the toolbar.

Your original photos are never changed. The app works from copies in `~/Messier Chart`.
